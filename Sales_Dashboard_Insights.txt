Sales Dashboard – Key Insights

1.  Europe is the top-performing region, contributing the highest share
    of total sales, followed by Asia, indicating strong market demand in
    these regions.

2.  Galaxy S is the leading revenue-generating category, significantly
    outperforming other product segments, making it the primary driver
    of overall sales performance.

3.  Sales show noticeable peaks during mid-year and the final quarter,
    suggesting seasonal purchasing patterns and potential promotional
    impact.

4.  The average customer rating of 3.73 indicates stable customer
    satisfaction, with opportunities for further improvement in product
    experience and service quality.
